Divinity & Nature

Guru Jambhoji & The 363 Khejarli Martyrs

In 1485 CE at Samrathal Dhora, Guru Jambheshwar Ji established Bishnoism to ensure human harmony with desert soil, trees, and animals.

In 1730 CE at Khejarli, when royal axemen were sent to fell green Khejri trees, Mata Amrita Devi Bishnoi embraced the trunk, speaking the eternal immortal vow:

"सिर सांटे रूंख रहे, तो भी सस्तो जाण।"

"If a tree is saved even at the cost of one's head, consider it a cheap bargain."

Following her courageous lead, 363 Bishnois embraced the axes and gave their lives to protect the sacred forest.
