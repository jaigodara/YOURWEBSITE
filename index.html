Founded in 1485 CE • Samrathal Dhora

The World's First Ecological Faith

Codified by Guru Jambheshwar Bhagwan through 29 sacred principles, Bishnoism represents five centuries of unwavering commitment to non-violence, wildlife sanctification, and environmental preservation.

363 अमर बलिदानी (Khejarli)

In 1730 CE, led by Mata Amrita Devi, 363 Bishnois sacrificed their lives hugging Khejri trees against royal woodcutters, igniting the world's first forest conservation movement.

जीव रक्षा एवं अहिंसा

Strict protection of the Blackbuck (काला हिरण), Chinkara, and all animals. Bishnoi communities protect sanctuary grounds where wildlife freely roams without fear.

29 नियम जीवन शैली

A complete synthesis of personal hygiene, compassion, spiritual discipline, vegetarianism, and ecological ethics handed down to cultivate pure living.
